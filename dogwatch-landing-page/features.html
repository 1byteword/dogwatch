03 // Control Plane

shape low-utility telemetry

Usage analytics plus cardinality intelligence decide what can be dropped, aggregated, or downsampled safely.

Decision Why It Is Safe Operator Outcome

Aggregate cardinal labels Impact preview shows no critical workflow breakage Series explosion contained
Downsample low-value streams SLO and alert fidelity preserved with coarse granularity Longer retention at stable cost
