Deep Insights. No Cardinality Tax.

Traditional monitoring crashes when you add too many tags. We built dogwatch for the messy, high-scale reality of the cloud.

BubbleUp Analysis

Don't guess why requests are slow. Select "bad" requests on a graph and we statistically compare them to "good" ones to find the exact outlier dimension.

Control Plane

Tame cardinality explosion. We aggregate unused labels and enforce quotas before storage, so you never get a surprise bill for high-cardinality metrics.

Exemplars & Traces

Bridge the gap between metrics and traces. Click any spike in your graph to see the exact trace IDs (Exemplars) that caused it.

No Magic. Just Physics.

We use eBPF to safely instrument the kernel. This allows us to observe all network traffic, syscalls, and function calls without modifying applications.
