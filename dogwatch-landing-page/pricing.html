Pricing FAQ

Subtle Interactive
What counts as an entity?+

An entity is a logical component: service, database, queue, host, or container role. Running 100 pods of the same service still counts as one service entity.

What happens when quota is exceeded?+

The control plane applies increasingly aggressive head sampling and shaping recommendations to keep spend bounded. Traffic is never blocked.

Do you support older kernels?+

Linux 4.14+ is the minimum. 5.8+ is recommended for deeper eBPF and uprobes coverage. Older environments can still ingest OTLP with reduced kernel-native visibility.

Can we migrate from Datadog without rebuilding everything?+

Yes. Migration Assistant imports dashboards, alerts, and SLOs with compatibility reports so teams can move incrementally and verify parity.
