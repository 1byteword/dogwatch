Protocol FAQ

What counts as an Entity?

An Entity is any logical unit: a Service, Database, or Queue. We do not charge by "Host" or "Container." Run 100 pods of the same service? That's 1 Entity.

What happens if I hit my quota?

The Control Plane will begin aggressive Head Sampling to keep you within budget. We never block traffic or crash your app. You will see a warning in the dashboard.

Do you support legacy kernels?

eBPF requires Linux 4.14+. If you are on an older kernel, our binary falls back to standard OTLP ingestion (port 4317), but you lose the "Zero Config" magic.
